This website serves as a DEMONSTRATION of what can be tracked through device fingerprinting, a technique that combines your browser settings, hardware configurations, and operating system details to uniquely identify you without relying on cookies or personal data. This site does not store your information in any way, nor does it allow you to store other people’s information. Your IP is processed using IPinfo.io, but everything else is done locally on your device. It makes use of UAParser.dev and Joe12387's incognito detector.
